# VECTRA Usage Policy

This document explains, in plain language, how VECTRA may and may not be used.

The formal license is provided in [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE). If there is any
conflict between this document and the license, the license controls.

## Summary

VECTRA is a source-available LaTeX CV and resume template.

You may use it to create your own CV or resume by editing the user-editable
content files. You may publish and share your generated CV or resume PDF without
visible attribution.

You may not redistribute, republish, mirror, repackage, sell, or create another
template project based on VECTRA.

If you want to share VECTRA with others, please cite or link to the official
repository:

```text
https://github.com/yixnhuang/vectra
```

## What You May Do

You may:

- Use VECTRA to create your own CV, resume, academic profile, or application
  document.
- Edit your personal information in `cv/profile.tex`.
- Edit document-level personal settings in `cv/settings.tex`.
- Edit CV content in files under `cv/sections/`.
- Add, remove, or reorder your own CV entries.
- Compile VECTRA to generate your own PDF.
- Submit your generated PDF for academic applications, job applications,
  internship applications, fellowship applications, research opportunities, or
  similar personal purposes.
- Upload your generated CV or resume PDF to your personal website.
- Send your generated CV or resume PDF to advisors, employers, admissions
  committees, collaborators, or recruiters.
- Use your generated PDF without visible attribution to VECTRA or Yixuan Huang.
- Recommend VECTRA by linking to or citing the official repository.

## What You Should Edit

Most users should only edit these files:

```text
cv/profile.tex
cv/settings.tex
cv/sections/*.tex
```

These files contain your name, contact information, CV entries, skills, awards,
projects, and other personal content.

## What You Should Not Edit

Most users should not edit:

```text
src/
cv/main.tex
examples/
docs/
Makefile
latexmkrc
```

The files in `src/` define the template design, layout, typography, spacing,
footer, header, section style, and entry formatting. They are part of the VECTRA
Template Source and may not be modified for redistribution or republication.

## What You May Not Do

You may not:

- Redistribute the VECTRA template source files.
- Upload a copy of VECTRA to another repository.
- Publish a modified version of the template.
- Create another CV or resume template based on VECTRA.
- Repackage VECTRA as part of a template collection.
- Sell VECTRA or a modified version of VECTRA.
- Use VECTRA in a paid resume/CV writing, consulting, recruiting, document
  preparation, or template service without written permission.
- Remove copyright, license, or attribution notices from the template source.
- Claim that VECTRA or its template design is your own work.
- Use the VECTRA name or branding to imply endorsement or affiliation.

## Generated CVs and Resumes

Your generated CV or resume belongs to you.

You do not need to display a visible credit, watermark, citation, or license
notice on your generated PDF.

You may publish, submit, print, email, or upload your generated CV or resume for
personal academic and professional purposes.

## Sharing VECTRA

If you want to share VECTRA with others, do not upload or repost the template
files.

Instead, link to or cite the official repository:

```text
Yixuan Huang. VECTRA: Versatile Experience and Career Template for Research and
Academia. GitHub repository: https://github.com/yixnhuang/vectra
```

## Commercial Use

Personal use of your generated CV or resume for academic applications,
professional applications, job applications, internship applications, fellowship
applications, and personal websites is allowed.

Commercial use of the template itself is not allowed without written permission.

Examples of prohibited commercial use include:

- Selling VECTRA.
- Selling a modified version of VECTRA.
- Including VECTRA in a paid template bundle.
- Using VECTRA in a paid resume builder.
- Using VECTRA to provide paid CV or resume services to others.
- Repackaging VECTRA as part of a commercial document generation system.
